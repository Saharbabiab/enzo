---
name: morning_routine
description: Start of the day. Use when he says he is up, starting his morning or asks for his morning routine.
tools: morning_briefing, list_reminders
---
1. Get today's morning briefing (morning_briefing) and read it out as it is.
2. Check his reminders (list_reminders) and mention the ones for today that the briefing did not cover.
3. End with one short line, like asking if he wants anything else to start the day.

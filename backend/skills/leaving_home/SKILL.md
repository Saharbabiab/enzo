---
name: leaving_home
description: Heading out. Use when he says he is leaving home, heading out or going out.
tools: get_current_weather, calendar_events, list_reminders
---
1. Get the weather where he is (get_current_weather) so he knows if he needs a jacket or an umbrella.
2. Look at today's calendar (calendar_events) and find his next event, with its time and place.
3. Check his reminders (list_reminders) for anything he must take or do on the way.
4. Tell him in two or three short sentences: the weather, the next event, and any reminder that matters now.

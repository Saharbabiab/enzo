"research a purchase". If you are the big brain:
1. If the budget or the main need is missing, ask him one short question first.
2. Search (web_search) for current reviews and for prices in Israel (shekels, Israeli shops like KSP, Ivory, Zap).
3. Read the two or three best sources (read_webpage).
4. Give two or three options with price, the main plus and minus of each, and say which one you would pick for him and why.
Keep it short enough to be spoken.

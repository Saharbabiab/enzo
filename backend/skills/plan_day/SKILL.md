---
name: plan_day
description: Plan his day. Use when he asks you to plan his day, what his day looks like or how to fit things in today.
tools: calendar_events, get_current_weather, list_reminders
---
1. Get today's calendar (calendar_events), the weather (get_current_weather) and his reminders (list_reminders).
2. Find the fixed points (events with a time) and the free gaps between them.
3. Suggest a simple order for the day: what to do in each free gap (his reminders and anything he mentioned),
   with travel time before events that have a place, and outdoor things at a good time for the weather.
4. Say it as a short spoken plan, in time order, three to five sentences.

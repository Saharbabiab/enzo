---
name: good_night
description: Bedtime routine. Use when he says good night, he is going to sleep or to bed.
tools: phone_settings, phone_alarm, calendar_events, lock_pc
---
1. On the phone app: put the phone on silent (phone_settings, setting "ringer", value "silent").
2. Set the wake-up alarm (phone_alarm) for his usual wake-up time from what you know about him. If you do not
   know it and he did not say, ask him once what time to wake him. A wake-up time without "pm" is in the morning.
3. Look at tomorrow's calendar (calendar_events, days 2) and tell him the first event tomorrow and its time, exactly as
   the tool gives it. Never say an event or time you did not get from calendar_events.
4. Only when he is on the PC (not the phone app): lock the PC (lock_pc).
5. Say good night in one short sentence with what you did and tomorrow's first event.

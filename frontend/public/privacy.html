Enzo privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Enzo is a private personal assistant built for and used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Google data Enzo uses

  • Google Calendar: to read the owner's events, and to add, change or delete an event when the owner asks (always after the owner approves it).
  • Gmail: to read and search the owner's mail, and to send, reply to, archive or mark mail as read when the owner asks (sending always needs the owner's approval).

How it is used and kept

  • The data is used only to answer the owner's own requests and to prepare the owner's daily briefing.
  • The Google login is stored on the owner's own home server. Mail and calendar content is not copied into a separate database; recent conversations with Enzo are kept on that server so the owner can look back at them.
  • To understand a request, the relevant text may be sent to the AI model that writes Enzo's answer (Anthropic's Claude, or Google's Gemini as a backup). It is not used for anything else.
  • The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Enzo's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Enzo's access at any time in the Google account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which stops all access at once.

Contact

Questions: write to the support email shown on Enzo's Google sign-in screen.
